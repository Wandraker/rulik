How to connect

Three steps and a couple of minutes. After that the phone reconnects on its own.

  1. Start Rulik Server on your PC

    The window shows the PC's address and a PIN. If there's a driver warning, press "Install driver" and restart the server: without the ViGEmBus driver only mouse and keyboard work. When Windows asks about the firewall, allow access on private networks.

  2. Install the app on your phone

    Download rulik.apk from the Download page and open it. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager.

  3. Connect and play

    Your PC shows up on the Connect tab. Tap it, enter the PIN from the server window, go to Layouts and press ▶. In the game's control settings, pick the gamepad.

Wi-Fi

The phone and the PC must be on the same network. The PC can be wired to the router, which is even better. Discovery uses a broadcast packet, so there's nothing to type. If the PC doesn't show up, enter the address from the server window by hand, for example 192.168.1.52.

Still can't connect? Press "Firewall" in the server window: it adds a rule for port 47800.

USB tethering

The easiest wired option, no debugging needed. Plug the phone into the PC and turn on "USB tethering" in Android settings (Hotspot & tethering). Keep the Wi-Fi tab in the app: the PC shows up in the list and the traffic goes over the cable. The PC doesn't need a Wi-Fi adapter for this.

Windows marks the phone's network as public and blocks incoming connections from it by default. The server notices this and shows a warning: press "Firewall" once and the rule works on every network.

If no new network shows up in Windows after you turn on tethering, open Device Manager: the phone should appear as "Remote NDIS based Internet Sharing Device". A different cable (not a charge-only one) or another USB port usually helps.

USB via adb

For people with USB debugging turned on. Install platform-tools or put adb.exe next to the server. Plug in the phone, press "USB (adb)" in the server window, then switch the app to USB and press "Connect via USB".

Layout editor

In the layout list press ⋮ and "Edit", or use "⋯" while playing. The plus button adds buttons, sticks, D-pads and touchpads. Drag an element to move it, pull the corner circle to resize it, and open its properties with the sliders button.

  • Button presses a gamepad button, a key, a combo of up to 4 keys, a mouse button or the wheel. Modes: hold, toggle, turbo.
  • Stick works as the left or right stick, WASD, arrows, D-pad or mouse. It can float to where your thumb lands.
  • Touchpad moves the mouse (tap, two-finger right click, scrolling) or turns the camera like a right stick.

Layouts are shareable: "Share" sends them as text, and "Import from clipboard" adds a copied layout. Ready-made ones are on the Layouts page.

Gyro and tilt

Turn on the gyro in the layout settings (the gear in the editor): it moves the mouse or the right stick. The "while held" mode works with a "Gyro while held" button, like aiming on a Switch. Tilt steering drives the left stick, and the full-turn angle is adjustable. If an axis goes the wrong way, turn on invert.

If it lags

  • Put the phone on 5 GHz Wi-Fi and wire the PC to the router.
  • Turn on the ping display in the app: 2–10 ms is normal. Over 30 means the network is the problem.
  • Try USB tethering: the cable adds almost no delay.
  • Turn off battery saver on the phone.
