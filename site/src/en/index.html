Control your PC from your phone

Rulik turns an Android phone into an Xbox gamepad, a mouse and a keyboard. The game sees a real controller, so it doesn't matter where you got it: Steam, Epic, GOG, a disc or anywhere else.

Download for free How to connect
{{icon:pad}}

Any game

A virtual Xbox 360 gamepad through the ViGEmBus driver. Works wherever a regular controller works.

{{icon:link}}

Wi-Fi or cable

UDP over Wi-Fi with a few milliseconds of latency. No Wi-Fi? Use USB through tethering or adb.

{{icon:layout}}

Your own layouts

Buttons, sticks, D-pads and a touchpad. Move them, resize them, bind keys and key combos.

{{icon:gyro}}

Gyro and tilt

Switch-style gyro aiming, tilt steering for racing games, and game rumble on your phone.

{{icon:pc}}

Play right on the phone

The PC's picture and sound go to the phone's screen, with your layout drawn on top. The game runs on the PC, so the phone plays anything your computer can. In Desktop mode you can control the PC's screen with your finger.

{{icon:keys}}

Even if the game has no gamepad support

On-screen buttons and sticks can be bound to keys and the mouse. The game thinks you're on a keyboard, so you can play strategy games, old games and anything else without gamepad support from your phone. No reWASD or JoyToKey needed.
