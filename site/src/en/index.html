Your phone instead of a gamepad and a monitor

Rulik turns an Android phone into an Xbox gamepad, a mouse and a keyboard for your PC. And when the monitor is far away, the PC's picture and sound go straight to the phone, so you can play from the couch.

Download for free How to connect

Free · no ads · Android 10+ and Windows 10/11 · Wi-Fi or cable

Two ways to play

{{icon:pad}}Phone as a gamepad

You look at the monitor, and the phone in your hands works as an Xbox gamepad, a mouse or a keyboard. The game thinks a real controller is plugged in, so it doesn't matter where you got it: Steam, Epic, GOG or a disc.

Ready-made layouts →

{{icon:pc}}Phone as a monitor

The PC's picture and sound go to the phone, with the layout drawn on top. The game runs on the PC, so the phone plays anything your computer can. The graphics card compresses the video, and latency over 5 GHz Wi-Fi is usually 20–40 ms.

How to play on the phone →

And the PC desktop in your pocket

The computer's screen on the phone, controlled with your finger: tap to click, hold for right click, two fingers to scroll. Start a game or pause a movie without getting off the couch.

What else it does

{{icon:keys}}

Games without gamepad support

On-screen buttons can be bound to keys and the mouse. That covers strategy games, old games and anything with no gamepad support at all. No reWASD or JoyToKey needed.

{{icon:layout}}

Your own layouts

Buttons, sticks, D-pads and a touchpad. Drag them around, resize them, bind keys, combos and turbo.

{{icon:gyro}}

Gyro and tilt

Gyro aiming like on the Switch, tilt steering for racing games and phone vibration from the game.

{{icon:link}}

Wi-Fi or cable

A few milliseconds of ping over Wi-Fi. No Wi-Fi? Use a wire: USB tethering or adb.

{{icon:phone}}

Fewer misses

A tap next to a button goes to the nearest one, the phone clicks with vibration at button edges, and button zones adapt to your fingers.

{{icon:pad}}

Two players, one PC

Up to eight phones, each with its own gamepad. A stranger's phone on the network can't connect without the PIN.

Getting started

1

Server on the PC

Download rulik-server.exe and run it. No install needed, and it offers the gamepad driver by itself.

2

App on the phone

Install rulik.apk on Android 10 or newer. About 3 MB.

3

Connect and play

The phone finds the PC on the network by itself. Enter the PIN from the server window and pick a mode.
