## Wired

Plug in a USB cable and Windows installs the driver itself. That's it.

## Bluetooth

Works with Xbox One S and newer, and with Series pads.

1. On the PC: Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device → Bluetooth.
2. Turn the gamepad on with the Xbox button, then hold the small pair button on top, next to the USB port, until the Xbox button blinks fast.
3. Pick "Xbox Wireless Controller" from the list.

## Xbox Wireless Adapter

Lower latency and up to 8 gamepads. Pairing works the same way: the button on the adapter, then the pair button on the gamepad.

> A wireless Xbox 360 pad needs its own receiver: it doesn't work over Bluetooth.
