## Connecting

Connect with a cable or over Bluetooth. To pair, hold these buttons until the light bar blinks:

- DualShock 4: Share and PS.
- DualSense: Create and PS.

Then on the PC: Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device → Bluetooth.

## The game supports PlayStation itself

Sony ports and many newer games understand PlayStation gamepads directly. Everything works right away, with the right button icons.

## Through Steam

In Steam, open Settings → Controller and turn on PlayStation support. Steam hands the gamepad to the game as an Xbox pad.

Works in Steam games. You can add other games via Games → Add a Non-Steam Game and launch them through Steam.

## Through DS4Windows

For games outside Steam. It creates a virtual Xbox gamepad through the ViGEmBus driver, the same one Rulik uses. If Rulik Server already installed the driver, you don't need to install it again.

> Don't run Steam and DS4Windows on the same gamepad at once: the game will see it twice and every press will register two times.
