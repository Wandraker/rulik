## How to connect

The phone and the PC must be on the same network. The PC can be wired to the router, that's even better.

1. Start Rulik Server on the PC.
2. In the app, open the Connect tab: the PC shows up under "PCs on the network".
3. Tap it and enter the PIN from the server window.

Discovery uses a broadcast packet on the local network, so there's nothing to type.

## Entering the address manually

If the PC isn't found, tap "Enter the address manually" and type the address from the server window, for example `192.168.1.52`. The server window may list several addresses: use the one that belongs to your home network.

> Still not found? Press "Firewall" in the server window: it adds a rule for port 47800. Other causes are in [PC not found](not-found.html).

## Making it faster

- Connect the phone to 5 GHz Wi-Fi and wire the PC to the router.
- Don't sit far from the router or behind thick walls.

More in [If it lags](lag.html).
