## Touchpad

The "Mouse & keyboard" layout is mostly a touchpad:

- **Tap**: left click.
- **Two-finger tap**: right click.
- **Two fingers up and down**: scrolling.
- **Tap then hold**: dragging windows and files.

Change the sensitivity in the touchpad's properties in the [editor](editor.html).

## Typing

A button with the "Open the keyboard" action brings up the phone's keyboard. Letters are sent to the PC right away as you type, so you can write in a browser, a messenger or a game chat.

## Keys and combos

Any button can press a key or a combo of up to 4 keys: Alt+Tab, Win, Ctrl+C, volume and play/pause media keys. A ready-made movie remote is in the "Media remote" layout.

> Mouse and keyboard work even without the ViGEmBus driver: it's only needed for the gamepad.
