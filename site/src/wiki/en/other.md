## Switch Pro

Connects with a cable or over Bluetooth (pair button on top). Turn on its support in Steam: Settings → Controller. Outside Steam most games won't understand it.

## 8BitDo and budget gamepads

They usually have two modes:

- **X-input**: pretends to be an Xbox gamepad. This is the one you want.
- **D-input**: the old DirectInput. In this mode many games don't see the gamepad or mix up the buttons.

The mode is set with a switch on the case or a button combo at power-on. Check the gamepad's manual for the exact combo.

## Testing

Press Win+R and type `joy.cpl`. In X-input mode the gamepad is usually called "Controller (XBOX 360 For Windows)" or similar. More in [How gamepads work](gamepads.html).
